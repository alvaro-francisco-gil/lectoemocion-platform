# *El gallo Rayo* page provenance

The 31 pages of the illustrated story *El gallo Rayo — ¿Cómo suenan las
letras?*: one cover and thirty letter pages, each a drawing and a recording of
the sound that letter makes.

Generated by `scripts/import-story-pages.mjs`. Re-run it to reproduce every
file here; do not add pages by hand.

## Source and rights

Imported from the `data/` directory of the standalone PHP application the book
shipped with, which is assessed in `docs/migration/gallo-rayo-app.md`.

The artwork and the recordings are from the published picture book *El gallo
Rayo — ¿Cómo suenan las letras?* by **Belén Gil**. The author is a co-founder
of LectoEmoción and has granted the product the right to ship them; the
repository owner attests to this. As with `../../vocabulary/`, no per-file
licence identifier was recorded, and the remedy if a file is ever challenged is
to replace it and re-run the importer.

No page depicts a real child, and none carries child data: the drawings are of
a rooster and other farm animals. The recordings are of an adult voicing
letter sounds. The source application had a feature for attaching a child's
photo and voice to a page — **none of that content was imported, and none of it
existed in the source folder**; its `data.json` was empty and its `uploads/`
directory held nothing. Personalisation in this product goes through the
panel's typed services, never through a file drop.

## Naming

Files are named by sequence position, zero-padded — `00` is the cover and
`01`–`30` are the letter pages, in reading order. They are deliberately *not*
named after the letter they teach. Which letter falls on which page is authored
content and it already has exactly one home, `letterSounds` in
`@lectoemocion/template-catalog`; putting it in a filename as well would give
that ordering a second answer that could drift from the first.

## Processing

Each page scan was 2000×1500. The player composites at a 1280×720 logical
resolution and lets classroom panels upscale (ADR 0003), so nothing above 1280
was ever going to reach a screen. Each was flattened onto white, resized to fit
within 1280 without enlargement, and re-encoded as WebP at quality 80 —
higher than the world backdrop's 72 because these are ink-on-white drawings,
where WebP's ringing shows on every outline, and because they are looked *at*
rather than looked past. The result is 1280×960 and about 30 kB a page: 3.5 MB
of JPEG became roughly 1 MB.

Unlike a character import, a page is neither trimmed to its content nor lifted
off its background. The white margin *is* the page, and the letter sits out in
it — flooding the edges to transparency would eat the letter, and trimming
would crop it away.

The recordings were copied byte for byte. AAC in an MP4 container plays in
every browser the player supports, so a transcode would spend generation loss
to change nothing — and these are a single human performance for a book that is
not being re-recorded. They are the larger half of the import at about
5 MB; if that becomes a problem on a school network the answer is to fetch
pages lazily, not to re-encode the voice.

## Why this directory carries a PROVENANCE.md

`scripts/check-privacy.mjs` requires one beside any photographic, audio, or
video file, so that an undocumented one is visible as either a privacy incident
or an asset whose usage rights nobody recorded.
